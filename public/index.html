Nox

Play games made with Nox Studio, with friends on your Wi-Fi or anywhere.

Docs: make games with Nox Studio

Windows

Download for Windows
  1. Unzip it anywhere (right-click, Extract All), then open nox.exe in the Nox folder.
  2. Windows may say it protected your PC, because Nox is new and not signed yet: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. If Windows asks about the firewall, click Allow, or games on your Wi-Fi won't show up.

Android

Download for Android
  1. Open the file once it's downloaded.
  2. Your phone will ask to allow installing apps from your browser (Nox isn't on the Play Store yet): allow it, then Install.

Nox language

The scripting language behind Nox, on its own. Lua with sandboxing, JSON and web requests. Free and open source (MIT).
